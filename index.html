About

I'm Matthew Fletcher, a post production manager and finishing editor with over 15 years of experience delivering broadcast-ready content for networks including ABC, Discovery, PBS, NOVA, and Smithsonian Channel. I founded House Productions in 2009 and currently lead post production at Lone Wolf Media, where I oversee editing, finishing, color, and audio workflows from raw footage to final delivery.

My work spans documentary and branded content, with a focus on technical precision and storytelling craft. Whether I'm cutting a story in Avid, conforming and mastering a network deliverable, grading in DaVinci Resolve, or building the workflows that keep a post team on schedule, I'm committed to elevating every project through both technical excellence and creative sensitivity.
