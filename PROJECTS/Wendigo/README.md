# WENDIGO — Prologue Storyboard

A visual storyboard for the prologue of *WENDIGO*, a novel by Noah Keates.

## Overview

This storyboard presents the cinematic visualization of the WENDIGO prologue, spanning five scenes across approximately 4 minutes of screen time. All frames are presented in 2.39:1 anamorphic aspect ratio.

## Scenes

| Scene | Title | Slugline | Frames |
|-------|-------|----------|--------|
| 01 | The Wall | EXT. HAILEY HILL WALL — DUSK | 1A–1D |
| 02 | The March | EXT. WOODS PATH — CONTINUOUS | 2A–2C |
| 03 | The Coin | EXT. CLEARING STREAMBED | 3A–3D |
| 04 | The Binding | EXT. CLEARING — BACK POST | 4A–4D |
| 05 | The Arrival | EXT. CLEARING — EDGE OF TREES | 5A–5E |

## Technical Specifications

- **Aspect Ratio:** 2.39:1 anamorphic
- **Color Palette:** Desaturated blues, grays, blacks. Warmth only from orange sunset and copper tones
- **Lighting:** Natural overcast winter, low-angle hard sunset backlight
- **Film Qualities:** Grain, anamorphic characteristics, slight lens flare
- **Era:** 1850s northern Maine logging settlement
